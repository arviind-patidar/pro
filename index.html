01

The Role of a Buyer-Side Consultant

What does a property consultant in Bangalore do?

A property consultant helps buyers make better property decisions, rather than simply showing them properties. This can include understanding requirements, researching the market, shortlisting properties, comparing projects and developers, evaluating locations and properties, assessing pricing, conducting due diligence, and supporting negotiation and closure.

What is the difference between a property consultant and a property broker?

A property broker typically facilitates a transaction or connects a buyer with available inventory. A property consultant can take a broader advisory role, helping the buyer evaluate the property, project, developer, location, pricing and risks before making a purchase decision.

Is acre&key a property broker or a property consultant?

acre&key is a buyer-side property consultancy and Home Buyer Concierge. We help buyers evaluate residential property, compare opportunities, assess risks, conduct due diligence, understand pricing and negotiate before they commit. We also work with developers as part of our commercial model.

What is a buyer-side property consultant?

A buyer-side property consultant focuses on the interests and objectives of the person purchasing the property. The role can include evaluating alternatives, challenging assumptions, identifying risks, assessing value and helping the buyer negotiate the purchase.

Why should I use an independent property consultant in Bangalore?

The property market involves an information imbalance. Developers know their projects, brokers know their transactions, and buyers are often expected to make a high-value decision while navigating competing opinions and sales narratives. A buyer-side consultant provides an additional layer of independent evaluation focused on the buyer's objectives.

What does a property buying consultant in Bangalore help with?

A property buying consultant can support the complete purchase journey, including requirement mapping, market research, property search, shortlisting, project and developer evaluation, property evaluation, due diligence, price analysis, negotiation and purchase support.

Pricing, Negotiation & Commercial Transparency

Can a property consultant help negotiate the price?

Yes. A buyer-side consultant can analyse pricing, project premiums, comparable market context, payment structures, inventory and other commercial considerations that may influence a negotiation. acre&key provides negotiation support as part of its buyer-side advisory process.

Can acre&key negotiate with developers on my behalf?

Yes. Where applicable, acre&key can support negotiations with developers around pricing, premiums, payment structures and other commercial terms, with the objective of helping the buyer secure better terms.

If acre&key is paid by developers, how is the advice buyer-side?

How does acre&key manage potential conflicts of interest?

acre&key's evaluation process is designed to assess opportunities against the buyer's requirements, pricing, alternatives, project and developer considerations, and relevant risks. Where a commercial relationship with a developer exists, that relationship should be disclosed to the buyer so the commercial context is clear.

Does acre&key recommend only properties from developers it works with?

No. The starting point is the buyer's requirements, not a developer's inventory. Opportunities are assessed based on suitability and the buyer's objectives. Where a developer relationship exists, the relevant commercial arrangement should be understood alongside the recommendation.

Can acre&key tell me not to buy a property?

Yes. The purpose of buyer-side advisory is not to make every shortlisted property work. What happens if acre&key disagrees with the property I want to buy?
