About Sumadhura Capitol Residences

A 24-acre master-planned residential precinct of ~2,000 homes within Hope Farm / ITPL Main Road, Whitefield, Bengaluru in Varthur.

Planned across 14 residential towers, the development balances premium high-rise architecture with expansive open spaces, dedicated lifestyle amenities, and shared township infrastructure.

The development is planned around:

Residential — multiple apartment configurations and layouts.
Open Spaces — approximately 70% reported open area.
Amenities — a clubhouse of approximately 86,000 sq.ft.
Masterplan — towers, common spaces, internal movement and shared facilities planned as one residential environment.

The scale of the development makes the masterplan and execution an important part of understanding the project.

What Gets Added to the Headline Price?

PROJECT INCLUSIONS
  • Infrastructure Charges: BESCOM electricity connection, BWSSB water line & STP
  • Clubhouse Membership: Lifetime access to ~86k sq.ft. dual clubhouse hub
  • Advance Maintenance: Mandatory 12–24 months deposit upon possession
  • Sinking Corpus Fund: One-time capital replacement reserve
UNIT-DEPENDENT PREMIUMS
  • PLC (Location Premium): Added for uninterrupted Varthur Lake or courtyard view
  • Floor Rise Charges: ~₹25–₹50/sq.ft. incremental rise per level
  • Covered Car Parking: Single slot (1–3 BHK) or 2 slots (4 BHK residences)
GOVERNMENT STATUTORY LEVIES
  • GST (5%): Applicable on entire under-construction construction value
  • Stamp Duty (~5.1%): Government duty as per Karnataka registration
  • Registration & Cess (~1.5%): Surcharge, BBMP/BMRDA cess & handling
Note: Charges vary by unit, floor height, orientation, and builder's prevailing milestone payment schedule.

Sumadhura Capitol Residences FAQs

Essential answers and due diligence clarity for prospective buyers.

Is Sumadhura Capitol Residences RERA registered? +
Yes. Sumadhura Capitol Residences is registered under Karnataka RERA.

Buyers should always review the official Karnataka RERA portal filing and verify sanctioned floor plans before executing booking agreements.
What is the total acquisition cost of buying a Sumadhura Capitol Residences apartment? +
The advertised base price is only one component of the total acquisition cost. Buyers must account for infrastructure/clubhouse charges (~₹8.5L–₹12.5L), covered parking slots (~₹4L–₹7.5L), floor-rise or preferential location charges (PLC), advance maintenance & corpus deposits, GST at 5%, and Karnataka Stamp Duty + Registration at 6.6%.

Use acre&key’s interactive True Cost Analysis section above to review itemized breakdowns for 1 BHK, 2 BHK, 3 BHK, and 4 BHK configurations.
What is the carpet-area space efficiency of Sumadhura Capitol Residences floor plans? +
Carpet efficiency across Evergreen ranges between 66.5% and 69.8% depending on typology. The 1 BHK unit (650 sq.ft. SBA) offers ~435 sq.ft. RERA carpet (66.9% efficiency), while the 3 BHK Premium (1,850 sq.ft. SBA) achieves ~1,270 sq.ft. RERA carpet (68.6% efficiency).

See the Floor Plan & Space Analysis section above for configuration-level dimension breakdowns.
Is Sumadhura Capitol Residences suitable for end-use living? +
Evergreen is an attractive option for end users seeking a master-planned township lifestyle with Sumadhura Group's construction quality, dual clubhouses (~86,000 sq.ft.), and direct access to top schools (Chrysalis, TISB, Greenwood High) within 15 minutes.

The main trade-off is the 30 December 2027 possession timeline and current peak-hour congestion at Varthur rotary during school commute hours.
What is the long-term investment case and rental yield expectation? +
The project offers a projected gross rental yield of 3.8%–4.4%, anchored by over 350,000 tech employees across Whitefield and Outer Ring Road. Capital appreciation is driven by upcoming arterial infrastructure including the Satellite Town Ring Road (STRR) and Peripheral Ring Road (PRR).

acre&key models a 5-year CAGR of ~15.7% from current launch pricing to delivery stabilization.
What are the key watch-outs and risks identified by acre&key? +
acre&key’s due diligence highlights four key operational watch-outs:

• Varthur Junction Bottleneck: Rush-hour commute times to Bellandur ORR can stretch to 45–65 minutes despite a 11 km distance.
• Civic Drainage Infrastructure: Road widening and secondary drainage lines along SH-35 remain works in progress.
• Cauvery Water Transition: Dependent on timely BWSSB Stage V feeder line distribution to eliminate tanker dependence.
• Township Shared CAM Charges: Common infrastructure upkeep must be clearly audited in the maintenance agreement.
What is the master plan and location advantage of Sumadhura Capitol Residences? +
Sumadhura Capitol Residences is the dedicated Phase 2 residential sector comprising 24 acres within the grand 107-acre integrated township master plan. While Phase 1 (28 acres) focused on ultra-luxury 3, 4, and 5 BHK units, Evergreen provides 1, 2, 3, and 4 BHK typologies with dedicated clubhouse access while enjoying shared township retail and open space infrastructure.
What due diligence should I verify before executing the booking agreement? +
Before paying booking advances, verify:

• Specific tower construction milestones linked to your payment plan schedule.
• Demarcated covered parking allocation (single vs tandem parking bay).
• Sanctioned carpet area in the agreement vs brochure marketing drawings.
• Clear clauses regarding interest rates on delays and developer exit covenants.
