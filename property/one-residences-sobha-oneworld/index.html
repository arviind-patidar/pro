About One Residences at SOBHA OneWorld

One Residences at SOBHA OneWorld is positioned as part of a large integrated township in the OMR / Greater Whitefield corridor. The proposition is less about immediate convenience and more about long-term township scale, construction quality and a broad residential ecosystem.

The supplied research highlights SOBHA's integrated construction platform, a large masterplan, multiple configurations and a global landscape identity built around five themed zones.

For buyers, this creates a clear trade-off: the project offers future scale and a wide choice of homes, but the surrounding catchment and township infrastructure will mature over a longer period across six RERA phases extending into 2032–2033.

INDEPENDENT ADVISORY VERDICT

acre&key Verdict

Future Township Scale. Long-Term Bet.

One Residences at SOBHA OneWorld is best viewed as a long-horizon township proposition. Its strongest attributes are SOBHA's execution capability, integrated planning and broad product choice. Its weakest points are the long delivery horizon, six-phase complexity, current ecosystem maturity and future competing supply.

Our View

✓ Buy for:
Long-term township vision + SOBHA construction reputation / integrated construction platform + Large integrated masterplan + Broad 1–4 BHK choice + Future community scale + Large clubhouse and amenity proposition.
! Be cautious about:
Longest delivery horizon among the projects assessed in the supplied research + Six-phase delivery complexity + Current social/rental catchment still maturing + No current walk-to-metro advantage + High-rise 45–46 floor towers + Large future resale pool + Competing supply.

SOBHA OneWorld FAQs

Essential answers for prospective buyers
What is the price of One Residences at SOBHA OneWorld? +
The supplied primary pricing table reports approximately ₹1.10–1.16 Cr for 1 BHK, ₹1.59–1.96 Cr for 2 BHK, ₹2.26–2.96 Cr for 3 BHK and ₹3.14–3.93 Cr for 4 BHK.
What configurations are available? +
1 BHK, 2 BHK, 3 BHK and 4 BHK.
What are the sizes? +
1 BHK: 734 sq.ft. · 2 BHK: 1,063–1,205 sq.ft. · 3 BHK: 1,510–1,820 sq.ft. · 4 BHK: 2,096–2,415 sq.ft.
What is the base price? +
₹13,555/sq.ft.
What are the additional charges? +
PLC is reported up to ₹888/sq.ft.; floor rise up to ₹570/sq.ft.; power/infrastructure ₹432/sq.ft.; deposit/corpus ₹300/sq.ft. Car parking and clubhouse are reported as included.
How close is the metro? +
The proposed Purple Line station is reported at approximately 4.3 km. It is not currently a walk-to-metro proposition.
How far is ITPL? +
The source markets ITPL at roughly 25 minutes off-peak.
When is possession? +
The supplied research describes a six-phase completion horizon extending from July 2032 to September 2033. The exact phase-specific RERA date should be verified before booking.
Is it good for end use? +
It is best suited to buyers comfortable waiting for a future township ecosystem and who value SOBHA execution, broad home choices and long-term community scale over immediate convenience.
Is it good for investment? +
It is more suitable as a long-horizon investment thesis than as a near-term rental play, given the source's weaker liquidity score and immature rental/social catchment.
What are the biggest risks? +
Long delivery horizon, six-phase complexity, current ecosystem maturity, high competing supply, high-rise lift/liveability considerations and future township dependency.
